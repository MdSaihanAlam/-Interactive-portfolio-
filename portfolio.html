    Research · Paper in preparation00:00:24:00

    Guava leaf and fruit disease

    Six architectures trained with one recipe on 3,300 leaf images and 902 fruit images, so the comparison is fair. Leaves are close to solved. Fruit is not.

    98.56–100%leaf accuracy across all six models
    78.63%best fruit accuracy, multimodal fusion (95% CI 70.8 to 84.8%)

    Before training: 139 near-duplicate images with conflicting labels were removed. A border-only classifier scored 81.8% against 16.7% chance, so backgrounds were removed too, and the leaf results held.

    ConvNeXtV2DINOv2MobileNetV2Multimodal fusionGrad-CAMMixUp / CutMix
